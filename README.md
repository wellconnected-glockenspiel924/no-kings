# 👁️ no-kings - See who owns the websites you visit

[![Download no-kings](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Release-blue)](https://github.com/wellconnected-glockenspiel924/no-kings/releases)

## 🔍 Purpose
No Kings reveals the ownership structure of the websites you browse. Large financial firms like BlackRock, Vanguard, and State Street hold significant shares in major corporations. This tool maps the website in your browser tab to its parent company. It shows how much stock these three firms own. It also identifies who holds actual voting control when these firms do not. Use this tool to understand the financial interests behind the digital content you consume daily.

## ⚙️ System Requirements
* Windows 10 or Windows 11
* A modern web browser like Chrome, Edge, or Brave
* Internet connection for real-time ownership lookups

## 🛠️ Installation Details
The installer places a small background service on your machine. This service allows the browser extension to communicate with our ownership database. You do not need to manage this service manually. Windows handles all background tasks automatically. If the installer asks for permission to make changes to your device, select Yes. This allows the application to integrate with your browser safely and effectively. 

## 💡 How to Use
Once installed, look for the small icon in your browser toolbar. If you do not see it, click the jigsaw puzzle icon usually located in the top right corner. Find No Kings in the list and pin it for easier access. 

When you visit a website, click the icon to see a report. The interface displays three main metrics:
* The name of the parent company.
* The combined percentage of shares held by the Big Three fund giants.
* The name of the entity with majority voting control. 

The color of the report changes based on the ownership concentration. A green indicator suggests diverse ownership. A red indicator alerts you that a high percentage of shares belongs to index-fund giants.

## 🌐 Data Accuracy
The application pulls information from public financial disclosures. We verify these sources every twenty-four hours to ensure the data remains current. While companies undergo structural changes, our database reflects the latest filings available to the public. If you encounter a page that shows no data, this means the parent company is a private entity or has not filed mandatory ownership reports.

## 🛡️ Privacy and Safety
This application cares about your privacy. It only scans the address of the website you visit to look up the parent organization. It does not track your browsing history or collect your personal data. The lookup process happens locally on your machine. We do not store records of the sites you visit on our servers. Your browsing habits remain your own. 

## 🔧 Troubleshooting
If the icon remains grey, check your internet status. The tool requires a connection to fetch ownership information. If the tool displays an error, try removing and reinstalling the extension through your browser settings. In rare cases, other ad-blocking software might interfere with the display. Try disabling other extensions temporarily to see if the report appears. Reach out through the repository issue tracker if you find a bug. Provide a clear description of the website where the error occurred.

## 📈 Understanding Ownership
Financial ownership often masks who makes the final decisions for a company. Large index funds hold many shares but often delegate voting power. No Kings helps you see through the complexity of modern business structures. By showing you the entities behind the website, you gain a clearer picture of corporate influence. This transparency helps you make informed choices about where you spend your time and money online.
